TSFix+ now works **on its own** on the current Steam version of Tales of Symphonia: TSFix, Special K and dgVoodoo are no longer needed. It smooths motion to your display's full refresh rate and includes the fixes TSFix used to provide.

**To install:** download `tsfixplus-1.0.0.zip` below (under Assets) and copy **`d3d9.dll`** and **`tsfixplus.ini`** into the game folder (in Steam: right-click Tales of Symphonia → Manage → Browse local files). That's it. Coming from TSFix or TSFix+ 0.9? See [Coming from TSFix](https://github.com/coreyvanderlaan/ts-fix-plus#coming-from-tsfix-or-tsfix-09) first.

### New in 1.0
- **No other mods needed.** TSFix+ is loaded by the game as its `d3d9.dll`.
- **Game fixes** (the ones TSFix provided that the official patches never made):
  - the game keeps running in the background, and **Alt+Tab works**;
  - fullscreen is a borderless window, windowed mode a borderless window;
  - the game's own stuttery 30 fps limiter is replaced by exact pacing;
  - **videos play smoothly**, without stutter or black blocks (the game's 60 Hz timer is created correctly, and the GPU is waited for while a video plays);
  - the **Zelos title achievement** can be unlocked.
- **Texture packs** in TSFix's format (`TSFix_Res\inject`, such as the 4x upscale pack) are loaded if you have them. `TexturePacks=0` in `tsfixplus.ini` turns them off.
- **Spell effects:** fixed a spell's ground circle that could flicker above the caster.
- **Steam Deck:** works, see the guide in the [README](https://github.com/coreyvanderlaan/ts-fix-plus#steam-deck) (two files and one launch option).
- **Licence:** now GPL-3 (it builds on TSFix's GPL-3 work). 0.9 was MIT.

### Recommended settings
- Set Windows' refresh rate to your screen's highest (a borderless window runs at the desktop's rate).
- Set the game's resolution to your screen's own resolution.
- On an HDR screen, turn **Auto HDR** off for `TOS.exe` (Settings → System → Display → Graphics).

### Known limitations
- About one game update (~33 ms) of extra display latency.
- Menus, skits and the battle results screen stay at 30 frames a second.
- Tested on Windows 11 at 144 Hz and 60 Hz with G-Sync, and on a Steam Deck OLED. FreeSync untested.

Found a problem? [Open an issue](https://github.com/coreyvanderlaan/ts-fix-plus/issues) with what you saw and where, your refresh rate, and `tsfixplus.log` from the game folder.
