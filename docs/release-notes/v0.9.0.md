First public release. Tales of Symphonia (Steam, PC) moves at your display's full refresh rate.

**To install:** download `tsfixplus-0.9.0.zip` below (under Assets), unzip everything into the game folder (in Steam: right-click Tales of Symphonia → Manage → Browse local files), close the game and double-click **`install.bat`**. `uninstall.bat` undoes it. Details, and manual steps, are in `INSTALL.txt` and the [README](https://github.com/coreyvanderlaan/ts-fix-plus#installing).

Requires [TSFix](https://wiki.special-k.info/SpecialK/Custom/TSFix). TSFix+ is an independent add-on, not made by TSFix's author.

### What it does
- The game keeps running at 30 updates a second, so speed, timing and cutscenes are unchanged.
- Between those updates, the game's own scene is redrawn for every refresh of your display, with the camera, scenery, characters, spell effects, the battle target marker and shadows placed partway between the game's last two updates.
- The HUD, menus, text and videos are left exactly as the game draws them.
- **F9** turns it on and off. `tsfixplus.ini` has one setting, `MaxFPS` (0 = your display's refresh rate).

### Known limitations
- About one game update (~33 ms) of extra display latency.
- Tested for roughly the first hour of the game at 144 Hz, and briefly at 60 Hz, on a G-Sync monitor (G-Sync on), in borderless fullscreen (TSFix's default). Exclusive fullscreen and FreeSync are untested.

Found a problem? [Open an issue](https://github.com/coreyvanderlaan/ts-fix-plus/issues) with what you saw and where, your refresh rate, and `tsfixplus.log` from the game folder.
