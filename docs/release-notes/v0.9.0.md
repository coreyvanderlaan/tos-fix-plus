First public release. Tales of Symphonia (Steam, PC) moves at your display's full refresh rate.

**Download `tosfixplus-0.9.0.zip` below (under Assets)** and follow `INSTALL.txt`, or the [install steps in the README](https://github.com/coreyvanderlaan/tos-fix-plus#installing). Requires [TSFix](https://wiki.special-k.info/SpecialK/Custom/TSFix).

### What it does
- The game keeps running at 30 updates a second, so speed, timing and cutscenes are unchanged.
- Between those updates, the game's own scene is redrawn for every refresh of your display, with the camera, scenery, characters, spell effects, the battle target marker and shadows placed partway between the game's last two updates.
- The HUD, menus, text and videos are left exactly as the game draws them.
- **F9** turns it on and off. `tosfixplus.ini` has one setting, `MaxFPS` (0 = your display's refresh rate).

### Known limitations
- About one game update (~33 ms) of extra display latency.
- Tested for roughly the first hour of the game, at 144 Hz, in borderless mode. Exclusive fullscreen, variable refresh (G-Sync/FreeSync) and 60 Hz displays are untested.

Found a problem? [Open an issue](https://github.com/coreyvanderlaan/tos-fix-plus/issues) with what you saw and where, your refresh rate, and `tosfixplus.log` from the game folder.
